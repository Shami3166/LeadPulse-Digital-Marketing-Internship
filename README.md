# LeadPulse — Digital Marketing Internship Project

## Project Overview

This repository contains my final Digital Marketing Internship project completed at Devnexes Digital Solutions.

The project focuses on developing a 30-day multi-channel growth marketing campaign for LeadPulse, a conceptual SaaS product designed to help businesses track leads, conversions, and marketing performance.

## Project Components

### 1. Customer Persona & Funnel Strategy

Defines the ideal customer profile, target personas, customer pain points, buying journey, and TOFU, MOFU, and BOFU funnel strategy.

### 2. 30-Day Organic Content Strategy

Contains a 30-day content plan for LinkedIn, Twitter/X, and blog marketing, including content topics, visual concepts, funnel stages, and calls to action.

### 3. Paid Campaign & Email Drip

Includes Google Ads and LinkedIn Ads strategies, audience targeting, campaign structures, ad copies, and a five-part email nurture sequence.

### 4. Analytics CAC & LTV Model

An Excel-based financial forecasting model calculating marketing budget, leads, customers, CAC, LTV, ROI, channel performance, and scenario projections.

All financial figures and campaign results in this project are illustrative planning assumptions created for internship learning and forecasting purposes. They do not represent actual campaign performance.
